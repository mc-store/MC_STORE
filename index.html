About MC STORE

Your place to discover Minecraft capes and connect with our team.

MC STORE was created for Minecraft players and cape collectors who want a clear, straightforward place to explore cape designs and ask about availability.

We know that buying digital items online requires care. That is why we aim to make product information easy to understand, keep our listings clear, and handle order questions directly through our Discord community.

Every listing shows the information currently available to us. Before placing an order, contact our team to confirm the cape, its availability, the final price, and the delivery details.

Whether you are searching for a rare design or simply exploring the collection, we are here to help you make an informed choice.
