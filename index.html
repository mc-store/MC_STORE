About MC STORE

A Minecraft cape store built around clear listings, careful checks, and a better shopping experience.

MC STORE is an independent shop for Minecraft cape collectors. Founded in December 2024, our goal is to make cape shopping clearer in a community where misleading listings and unreliable sellers can be a concern.

We aim to make browsing and purchasing easier by showing clear product details and checking cape-code listings before they are offered. Please review each cape’s current stock, price, and delivery information so you can make an informed decision.

Community feedback matters to us. Our Endorsements page brings together creator features, customer-review information, and partner details as each item is confirmed.

MC STORE is not affiliated with Mojang, Minecraft, or Microsoft. Before placing an order, confirm the cape’s availability, final price, and delivery details. For questions or community updates, contact us through our Discord server.
